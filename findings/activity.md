# Findings: Activity

Activity topic (snapshot 2026-09-29)

Established:
- 11 sample tasks and 3 sample events came from org setup (creator 005Kc0000010gjJIAQ, 2025-01-20); they are excluded everywhere. That leaves 102 real tasks and 10 real events.
- 56 of the 102 real tasks are "Follow-up after Presentation" reminders made by the flow Opportunity_AS_Follow_up_Task. Each is due the day it was created, 51 are open and overdue, and most sit on opportunities that are already closed. Because the flow fired during the opportunity bulk load, it caused the May 2025 task spikes.
- 31 open next-step tasks were loaded on 2026-07-08 ET (07-09 UTC). None has a due date, so metrics dated by due date never count them.
- TaskRelation and EventRelation only repeat each activity's primary Who and What (the contact/lead and the related record), so joins don't double count. Every task on an opportunity has the same owner and account as that opportunity.
- Role: UserRoleId is never filled and Title is nearly empty, so the owner's profile is the only role signal. It is used as a proxy through Owner.ProfileId, with profile ids mapped to names in the notes.
- Events: ActivityDate is the start date in the org time zone (2 of 10 events fall on a different UTC date). Event Type is empty and all 10 real events are in 2026. Two zero-length "Audio" notes on a record that isn't an opportunity are excluded.

Metrics drafted and passing (FY2025 value, all checked by hand with SQL):
- tasks_completed: 8
- tasks_by_due_date: 54
- meetings_held: 0
- total_activities (derived, tasks_by_due_date + meetings_held): 54
- opportunities_with_activity: 49. Opportunities are identified by the WhatId key prefix 006 because the What relationship can't be joined. Only 2 of these opportunities have a task a person created.
- accounts_with_activity: 18. Only 3 of these accounts have a task that isn't the automated follow-up.

Open:
- Question: should the automated follow-ups count as activity? Default: included, with a caveat in each metric.
- Question: is profile the right role proxy? Default: Owner.ProfileId.
- None of the metrics were reconciled to the org's reports, because those reports are grouped by person or dated by Account.CreatedDate or CloseDate.
- No open or overdue task backlog metric was drafted, because the automated follow-ups make it misleading until the first question is answered.

## Claims

- **anomaly** (high) Salesforce sample data: 11 tasks (10 with "(Sample)" in the subject) and 3 events were created on 2025-01-20 by the org-creating user 005Kc0000010gjJIAQ at org setup; this user created no other activity. Excluding CreatedById = 005Kc0000010gjJIAQ leaves 102 real tasks and 10 real events. — `q37`, `q7`
- **fact** (high) 56 tasks have the subject "Follow-up after Presentation <opportunity>" and are generated by the flow Opportunity_AS_Follow_up_Task ("create follow-up task on opportunity presentation"). All 56 have a due date equal to their creation day, they cover 54 opportunities (2 opportunities got a duplicate), only 5 were completed, and 51 are open and past due. — `q11`, `q40`
  > Reviewer: q40 supports: 56 tasks, all due on their creation day (org time zone), 54 distinct WhatIds, 5 completed, 51 open and past due as of 2026-09-29. Two caveats. (1) 56 − 54 = 2 extra tasks does not prove 'two opportunities got a duplicate'. q20 shows 11 tasks on 9 Closed Lost opportunities, which could equally be one opportunity with 3 tasks. (2) None of the cited queries shows that the tasks come from the flow Opportunity_AS_Follow_up_Task; q11 shows 5 different creators. Cite the flow metadata, or say the source is inferred.
- **anomaly** (high) Bulk load of 31 open tasks by one user at 2026-07-08 in the org time zone (2026-07-09 UTC): none has a due date, each sits on a different opportunity (31 opportunities, 9 owners), and the subjects read as next-step notes, not activity that happened. The flagged 2025-05-13 load (20 tasks, plus 15 on 2025-05-14) is the presentation follow-up flow firing during the opportunity bulk load. — `q7`, `q11`
  > Reviewer: The 2026-07-08 part is supported: 31 open tasks, no due dates, 31 distinct WhatIds, and q20 shows they are all opportunities, with 9 owners. The second sentence is not. q7 shows the 05-13 and 05-14 tasks are all 'Follow-up after Presentation' reminders (the MIN and MAX subjects share that prefix), but no query looks at when opportunities were created, so 'firing during the opportunity bulk load' is unsupported. Show an opportunity CreatedDate or LastModifiedDate spike on those days, or drop the causal claim.
- **fact** (high) Every task linked to an opportunity has the same owner and the same account as that opportunity. The flow follow-up tasks are mostly open on opportunities that are already closed (27 at Project In Progress, 14 at Project Completion, 11 at Closed Lost, all Master_Opportunity), so "open tasks" is mostly stale reminders. — `q20`
- **fact** (high) TaskRelation and EventRelation only repeat each activity's primary WhoId/WhatId (task: 104 what + 21 who relations, all matching the primary; event: 11 + 9, all matching). No activity is shared across several contacts or records, so joining activities to opportunities or accounts does not double count. — `q24`
- **caveat** (high) Role cannot come from the User role or title: UserRoleId is never filled, and only 1 standard user has a Title. Profile is the only role signal. Task owners by profile: Standard User (9 owners, 44 tasks), Client Service - Manager (active 2 owners, 39 tasks; inactive 2 owners, 6 tasks), Creative Services - Manager (1 owner, 7 tasks), System Administrator (the rest). — `q14`, `q65`
  > Reviewer: The owner counts (113 in total) include the 11 sample tasks; q14 has no CreatedById filter. The inactive System Administrator's 11 tasks are very likely the sample data, so 'System Administrator (the rest)' mostly means demo records. Either state that sample tasks are included or re-run excluding 005Kc0000010gjJIAQ. Also, neither q14 nor q65 selects UserRoleId, so 'UserRoleId is never filled' has no evidence here.
- **fact** (high) In FY2025, 49 distinct opportunities have a non-sample task due in the year (52 tasks). 48 of them have a flow-generated "Follow-up after Presentation" task and only 2 have a human-created task, so opportunity activity coverage in 2025 is almost entirely the automated reminder. — `q115`
- **fact** (high) Event ActivityDate is the start date in the org time zone (America/New_York): it matches for all 10 real events, while 2 of the 10 start on a different UTC date. Real events: 10, all dated 2026. The Event Type field is never filled and EventSubtype is always Event. — `q93`, `q60`
  > Reviewer: q93 supports: ActivityDate equals the start date in America/New_York for all 10 events, and 2 of the 10 start on a different UTC date. q60 supports: all 10 are dated 2026, and EventSubtype is 'Event' for these 10. No cited query reads Event.Type, so 'Type is never filled' is unsupported. Also note that 2 of the 10 'real' events are zero-length Audio notes on a09 records.
